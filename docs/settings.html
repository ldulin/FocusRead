FocusRead is installed

  1. Open a paper, then click the FocusRead icon (or press Alt+R).
  2. Click any sentence to hear it. Space plays and pauses, J and K move a sentence at a time.
  3. Select a word or sentence to see a translation. B shows one under every sentence.
  4. For a PDF or Word file on your computer, use Open a PDF or Word file in the popup.

Translation defaults to Chrome's on-device engine: free, private, and it works offline once the language pack downloads.
